Assumptions

  • Prices are mid-2026 estimates for one person, furnished, in USD. "Studio" means a private air-conditioned room with its own bathroom; "Quiet condo" means a high-floor 1-bed with pool and gym in the building (the gym knob is then free).
  • Rent premiums: 1-month stays +30%, 2 months +15%, 3–5 months +8%, 6+ months list price.
  • Visa cost per month = fees plus border-run flights spread over the stay. Thailand assumes the 30-day exemption (from 15 Sep 2026) plus one 30-day extension (~1,900 THB), then a run.
  • Price drift compounds monthly. USD strength above 0 makes everything local cheaper.
  • Runway ends when cash hits your keep-back floor, not zero. The floor is your flight home plus a cushion.
  • Remote work for foreign clients on a tourist entry is a grey zone almost everywhere here. Thailand's DTV is the clean option that explicitly covers it.
  • Sources: TAT: Thai 30-day exemption · Thai embassy: DTV · Vialto: DE Rantau pause · Vietnam 90-day e-visa · Fragomen: Indonesia E33G · Cambodia e-visa switch · Philippines DN visa status
