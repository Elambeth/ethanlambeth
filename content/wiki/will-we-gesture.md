## A touchless future

I wonder if the future is where we don't touch things much. Aside from people. You won't touch screens, you won't open doors.

If you don't have to hold a phone, and all of your gestures are camera recognized then holding/touching devices will be gauche.

Touching a keyboard no, touching a phone screen no. God forbid someone else's.

If biorisk or health is slow and tech goes faster, that makes a lot of sense: germaphobe culture (especially if another pandemic).

The only way to know you are talking to a person is to shake their hand.
