What shape will swarms of agents take?

Will they follow a human organizational structure? Such as a company with middle managers? layers of delegation? Beaurocratic structures to maintain order? 

I guess there is an argument to be made that humans have accomplished a fair bit with the structure, so following them as to other structures within the animal kingdom would be wise. 


What else is there?

## Ant colonies?
A queen? Workers, roles, tasks. 

## A cell structure? 

Different enzymes serving different roles (are they intelligent enough to make this comparison?)

What about the process of DNA replication? that seems like extremely bespoke intelligence that is optimized for a single small task, this may not be how collections of agents organize themselves. 

(again note whether or not we will see multiple instances of agents cooporating. Competing etc) or will we just have "Mega agent" that does everything for us? -- but surely there will be different instances of it for different people and orgs? so even if anthropic eats the future there is still going to need to be some kind of scheduling/planning going on.
