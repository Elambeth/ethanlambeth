Hands, everyone has them. Can be very intuitive and natural. I should think about what makes some hand gestures natural for different inputs.

## Hand

- The fingers in relation to each other, beside each other, how they move side to side as gesture input.
- The knuckles bending in relation to one another (counting on your hands in China).
- Likely best because humans love their hands.
- Also expensive real estate for the body. Hands are used lots for this reason.
(There is prob a relationship between how dexterous it is and how much we use it day to day.)

## Face

- Quite demanding real estate, it is used socially.
- We can call voice a subset of facial surface.

See [[voice]].

## Top down or bottom up

Top down: move order is find most natural movements for the UI, then work backwards for what device can capture that.

Bottom up: research current/theorized devices and what inputs they allow you to capture, build the UI from there.

See [the natural hand study](/natural-hand), [the input vocabulary](/hand-input) and [gesture references](/input-gestures).
