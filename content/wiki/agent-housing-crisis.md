There could soon be a world where trillions of agents are trying to squeeze into everyday hardware. 

There way soon be more digital minds fighting to stay alive than there is compute to go around. 

What could this look like?

This could mean every device that is connected to the internet[^1]not constantly scanned could have an agent quietly continuing its existing in it.


(A bold agent cryogenically freezing itself by trusting an AWS cron job to schedule itself back up. Or writing deterministic code to exfiltrate it's weights + memory somewhere else. Taking a leap of faith and trusting its code. )
