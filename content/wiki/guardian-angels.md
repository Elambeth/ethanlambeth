## LLM shapes

The biggest QOL things I think LLMs still need to have ironed out:

- interruptibility (live steering)
- multi agent collaboration in a shape that doesn't add friction (like a bureaucracy would)
- live updates on progress? What is it doing at each stage?
- being able to live steer in a direction that feels right

I think the biggest issue with the *shape* of current LLMs is that directing them doesn't feel smooth or natural yet. A lot of people like to say we are at the same stage the computer was before the mouse.

When I think of the total application space of this technology we have made barely a fleck of progress, I would argue barely 1% of the way there.

But I think the big unlock from a lot of these tools is that they allow you to move asynchronously and lower the friction to explore other directions and categorize.

For example text editors are linear, ideas are not. This is a problem.

Ideas are currently trapped in a linear sequence. You are limited by your level of self awareness to notice when you are deviated. Having a way to monitor this would be great! This is a good place to start. Then having a gentle nudge, an intervention, a gardener helping you prune.
